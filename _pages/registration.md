Following the SSVM tradition, the registration fee is all-in, and will include:

<ul>
  <li>
    Hotel stay at Koningshof for 4 nights from Sunday to Thursday.
  </li>
  <li>
    Breakfast, lunch and dinner are included from Sunday's dinner to Thursday's lunch.
  </li>
  <li>
    Excursion and gala dinner on Wednesday. 
  </li>
</ul>

The registration fee will be 995€ for PhD students and 1195€ for other attendees.

The conference will be limited to 140 attendees.

Registration will open after notification of acceptance of submissions on the 28<sup>th</sup> of February 2027.
